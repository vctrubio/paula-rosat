# Retrato de Paula — segunda versión

Creado con la herramienta integrada de generación de imágenes, utilizando la fotografía proporcionada como referencia. El resultado original se conserva en `paula-sketch-v2.png`.

La versión web está en `public/portraits/paula-sketch-v2.svg`: trazados vectoriales obtenidos mediante separación de colores y Potrace, sin una imagen de mapa de bits incrustada. La conversión simplifica la textura fina del lápiz. El componente de retrato la coloca dentro del óvalo existente.

Solo se ha inspeccionado la ilustración independiente. No se ha ejecutado la aplicación, build, lint ni pruebas del proyecto.

## Prompt

Use case: style-transfer, identity-preserve. Reference: attached photograph of Paula; preserve her recognizable facial proportions, slightly turned head, eye spacing, high forehead, nose, asymmetric subtle closed-mouth smile, natural age, pulled-back dark hair with flyaway strands, curved gold earrings. Create a refined artist's pencil and fine sepia ink portrait for her website. The previous diagrammatic portrait looked artificial; this must have a convincing human likeness, sensitive anatomy, beautifully varied fine hatching and open unfinished contours. The face should remain mostly light warm ivory paper with understated graphite shadows, no thick outlines, no cartoon, no photorealistic color skin, no generic idealized woman. Her hazel eyes are the focal point: restrained luminous AMBER and honey irises, dark pupils and small natural highlights, keeping actual iris size. Very subtle muted amber on earrings. Background uniform warm ivory #f7f4eb without the vehicle interior, no frame, no botanical decorations, no lettering or watermark. Vertical composition matching reference, entire hair and chin visible, head and upper shoulders with a little breathing room, suited to an oval crop. Artwork should use a restrained palette, clean pencil contours and deliberate hatching instead of photographic grain, suitable for subsequent vector tracing. Produce only the portrait artwork.
