# Paula junto al alambique

Ilustración para el centro «Percibir relaciones». Generada con la herramienta integrada de imágenes a partir de la nueva fotografía de Paula junto al alambique, usando el retrato de portada como referencia de estilo.

Original: `docs/artwork/paula-alambic.png`. Versión de trazados SVG: `public/portraits/paula-alambic.svg`, mediante separación de colores y Potrace. Se conserva el retrato anterior en la portada.

## Prompt

Use case: identity-preserve / style-transfer. Create an original fine pencil and sepia ink sketch from reference image 1 (Paula standing beside a copper distillation still). Reference image 2 is STYLE ONLY: match its delicate line work, natural anatomy, warm ivory paper, soft crosshatching and restrained amber accents. Preserve Paula's recognizable face, age, gentle closed-mouth smile, head angle, hazel eyes and loose swept-back hair from image 1, not the hairstyle or pose from image 2. Keep the copper alembic beside her, with its rounded hammered body, swan-neck tube and small round gauge; it is central to this composition. Paula at left and still at right. Reframe as a balanced square vignette intended to fit inside a circular softly faded website illustration: keep her entire face and the whole recognisable body and neck of the still within the central 80 percent of the square, with breathing room around the pair. Upper torso only, simplify her patterned garment into sparse pencil marks. Replace the dark kitchen wall with open warm ivory paper #f7f4eb; only suggest a few kitchen contours very lightly. Mood: intimate artisanal portrait with her working tool. Amber-hazel irises and muted copper-gold still, mostly sepia/graphite otherwise. Avoid a photographic rendering, heavy dark background, cartoon features, thick outlines, clutter or decorative botanical additions. No text, no frame, no lettering, no watermark. Deliberate clean sketch contours and light hatching suitable for later SVG tracing.
